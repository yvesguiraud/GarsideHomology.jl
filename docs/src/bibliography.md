# Bibliography

## Garside theory and resolutions

- P. Dehornoy, F. Digne, E. Godelle, D. Krammer, J. Michel, *Foundations of Garside Theory*, EMS Tracts in Mathematics 22, European Mathematical Society (2015), [doi:10.4171/139](https://doi.org/10.4171/139). Germs of Garside monoids (chapter VI).
- P. Dehornoy, L. Paris, *Gaussian groups and Garside groups, two generalisations of Artin groups*, Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society (3) 79(3) (1999), 569–604, [doi:10.1112/S0024611599012071](https://doi.org/10.1112/S0024611599012071).
- P. Dehornoy, Y. Lafont, *Homology of Gaussian groups*, Annales de l'Institut Fourier 53(2) (2003), 489–540, [doi:10.5802/aif.1951](https://doi.org/10.5802/aif.1951). The order resolution, of which the lcm resolutions are variants.
- R. Charney, J. Meier, K. Whittlesey, *Bestvina's normal form complex and the homology of Garside groups*, Geometriae Dedicata 105 (2004), 171–188, [doi:10.1023/B:GEOM.0000024696.69357.73](https://doi.org/10.1023/B:GEOM.0000024696.69357.73).
- C. Squier, *The homological algebra of Artin groups*, Mathematica Scandinavica 75(1) (1994), 5–43, [doi:10.7146/math.scand.a-12500](https://doi.org/10.7146/math.scand.a-12500). Resolutions of the Artin monoids, an approach that the order resolution of Dehornoy and Lafont generalises.
- O. Garnier, *Generalization of the Dehornoy–Lafont order complex to categories. Application to exceptional braid groups*, [arXiv:2302.10033](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.10033). The tables of the homology of the exceptional braid groups with coefficients in ℤ and ℤε (tables 3 and 4).

## Braid monoids and reflection groups

- D. Bessis, *The dual braid monoid*, Annales scientifiques de l'École Normale Supérieure (4) 36(5) (2003), 647–683, [doi:10.1016/j.ansens.2003.01.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ansens.2003.01.001).
- D. Bessis, *Finite complex reflection arrangements are K(π, 1)*, Annals of Mathematics (2) 181(3) (2015), 809–904, [doi:10.4007/annals.2015.181.3.1](https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2015.181.3.1). The dual braid monoids of the well-generated complex reflection groups, and the Hurwitz action on the reduced decompositions of a Coxeter element.
- T. Brady, C. Watt, *A partial order on the orthogonal group*, Communications in Algebra 30(8) (2002), 3749–3754, [doi:10.1081/AGB-120005817](https://doi.org/10.1081/AGB-120005817). The criterion used to enumerate the simples of the dual braid monoids of the real groups.
- T. A. Springer, *Regular elements of finite reflection groups*, Inventiones Mathematicae 25 (1974), 159–198. The regularity of the Coxeter elements.
- D. Armstrong, *Generalized noncrossing partitions and combinatorics of Coxeter groups*, Memoirs of the American Mathematical Society 202, no. 949 (2009). The Fuss–Catalan numbers of multichains of simples, used in the checks of the germs.
- G.-C. Rota, *On the foundations of combinatorial theory I. Theory of Möbius functions*, Zeitschrift für Wahrscheinlichkeitstheorie und verwandte Gebiete 2 (1964), 340–368, [doi:10.1007/BF00531932](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00531932). The crosscut theorem, for the Möbius function in the certificates.

## Homology of complex braid groups

- F. Callegaro, I. Marin, *Homology computations for complex braid groups*, Journal of the European Mathematical Society 16(1) (2014), 103–164, [doi:10.4171/JEMS/429](https://doi.org/10.4171/JEMS/429).
- I. Marin, *Homology computations for complex braid groups II*, Journal of Algebra 477 (2017), 540–547, [doi:10.1016/j.jalgebra.2017.01.044](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2017.01.044).
- M. Salvetti, *The homotopy type of Artin groups*, Mathematical Research Letters 1(5) (1994), 565–577, [doi:10.4310/MRL.1994.v1.n5.a5](https://doi.org/10.4310/MRL.1994.v1.n5.a5).

## Software used

- J. Michel, *The development version of the CHEVIE package of GAP3*, Journal of Algebra 435 (2015), 308–336, [doi:10.1016/j.jalgebra.2015.03.031](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2015.03.031); its Julia version, [Chevie.jl](https://github.com/jmichel7/Chevie.jl), builds the reflection groups and their braid monoids.
- C. Fieker, W. Hart, T. Hofmann, F. Johansson, *Nemo/Hecke: Computer Algebra and Number Theory Packages for the Julia Programming Language*, in Proceedings of ISSAC '17, ACM (2017), 157–164, [doi:10.1145/3087604.3087611](https://doi.org/10.1145/3087604.3087611). AbstractAlgebra.jl, of the same project, gives the rings of coefficients.
- [SparseElementaryDivisors.jl](https://github.com/yvesguiraud/SparseElementaryDivisors.jl) computes the elementary divisors of the differentials.
