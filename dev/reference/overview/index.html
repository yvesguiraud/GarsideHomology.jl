Overview

The reference gathers the docstrings layer by layer: Germs, Orderings, Cells, Resolutions and certificates, Coefficients and homology, the generic layer Complexes, and the Chevie extension.

Each page says which of its names are exported, and so available after using GarsideHomology, and which are public only: those are written GarsideHomology.word, or imported by using GarsideHomology: word. The header of each docstring shows the full name, whatever its status.

GarsideHomology.GarsideHomology — Module
GarsideHomology

Small free resolutions of Garside monoids and their homology.

GarsideHomology computes the reduced lcm resolution of a Garside monoid, a variant of the Dehornoy–Lafont resolution with far fewer cells, and the homology of the corresponding groups with various coefficients.

This version is a package skeleton: the layers (germs, orderings, cell tree, projection, resolution and certificates, modules, complexes) are added step by step. Present so far: the generic layer of complexes, GarsideHomology.Complexes, whose public names are re-exported, the interface of germs (AbstractGerm), tabulated and lazy germs (TableGerm, CoxeterGerm), the orderings (LCMOrdering), the tree of the cells (CellTree), the projection onto the cells (LCMProjectionData) and the lcm resolution (lcm_resolution, LCMResolution), with its snapshots (write_snapshot) and its certificate (certify), the modules of coefficients (TrivialModule, SignModule) and the tensor products by them (tensor_product, homology(R, M)), in which a ring stands for its trivial module (ℤ: homology(R, ℤ), ℤ ⊗ R ⊗ ℤ; ℤε for the sign module). The elements of a germ are shown to the user as words in the atoms (GermElement: G(1, 2, 1), G("121")), with the settings of the display of Notation.
