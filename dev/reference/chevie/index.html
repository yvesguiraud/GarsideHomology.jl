Chevie extension

Loading Chevie.jl, by using Chevie, loads this extension. The methods it adds are documented with the functions they extend: germ, TableGerm, dual_germ, lcm_resolution and homology for the groups and monoids of Chevie, and the methods of the functions ⊗, word, elements, basis and rank of Chevie for the objects of GarsideHomology. The docstring of the extension describes how the simples are enumerated.

GarsideHomologyChevieExt.GarsideHomologyChevieExt — Module
GarsideHomologyChevieExt

Package extension loaded together with Chevie: the germs of the braid monoids (BraidMonoid of a finite Coxeter group) and of the dual braid monoids of Chevie, built by GarsideHomology.germ(M) and GarsideHomology.TableGerm(M), and GarsideHomology.dual_germ(W; c), which builds the germ of a dual braid monoid from the group without building the monoid. Also germ(W) for a finite Coxeter group (the germ of its classical braid monoid), lcm_resolution(M) and homology(M) for a monoid of Chevie, through its germ, and the methods of the ⊗ of Chevie for our complexes, and of its word, elements, basis and rank for our objects.

The right covers σ → σa given to GarsideHomology.tabulate come from the arithmetic of the group alone. An element is identified by the images of the roots of the generators, which form a basis: a key of n small integers, updated by the action of the atoms without forming products of permutations.

  • Braid monoid: search of the Cayley graph of the generators.
  • Dual braid monoid of a real group (2 among the degrees): search by layers with the criterion of Brady and Watt, σa is simple if and only if the root of a lies in the moved space of the Kreweras complement σ⁻¹c, by exact linear algebra.
  • Dual braid monoid of a complex group: prefixes of the Hurwitz orbit of a reduced decomposition of c (Bessis), then covers by the layers.
  • Reference: the simples listed by left_divisors of Chevie (method = :chevie).
