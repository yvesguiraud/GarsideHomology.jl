Orderings, certificates and snapshots

Orderings

An LCMOrdering fixes the cells of an lcm resolution: at each lcm met by the construction, a preorder on the lcms with the atoms decides which atoms give cells, ties keeping the order of the atoms at the root.

R = lcm_resolution(G)                                         # the reduced resolution
D = lcm_resolution(G, LCMOrdering(TrivialPreorder()))         # Dehornoy–Lafont, order 1:N
D′ = lcm_resolution(G, LCMOrdering(TrivialPreorder(); root_order = p))   # for the order p
  • ComplementDeglex, the default, orders the lcms by their complements, the shorter first, then by smallest word; it gives the reduced resolution.
  • TrivialPreorder ties all the lcms: the construction then keeps the order of the root everywhere, and gives the order resolution of Dehornoy and Lafont for that order.
  • FunctionPreorder takes any function, to explore other preorders. A preorder that is not compatible with divisibility may give a resolution that is not reduced, which is to be checked by its certificate; extends_divisibility tests the compatibility.

ncells(R) and ncells(R, n) give the numbers of cells, to compare two orderings.

Certificates

certify(R) checks the resolution and returns a ResolutionCertificate, shown as a short report:

  • correctness: homogeneity of the boundaries, ∂² = 0 computed in the bimodules with the products of the germ, and Euler characteristic of the cells of each weight against the Möbius function μ(1, ω);
  • minimality, the absence of terms 1·y·1 in the differential, and linearity, every term σ y τ having ℓ(σ) + ℓ(τ) = 1.

is_correct, is_minimal and is_linear read the report. A failed check is reported, never thrown. The check of ∂² = 0 is the costliest: certify(R; square_zero = false) skips it, and nworkers sets the number of tasks it uses. The Möbius function is computed by the crosscut theorem of Rota up to 16 atoms, and by a pass over the intervals above (keyword mobius).

Snapshots

write_snapshot(path, R) writes a resolution in a compressed text format (path ending in .txt.gz), with its cells and its differential, and by default its fingerprints beside it (.sha.csv). This is the format of the reference resolutions of the tests; it is described in the file test/data/reference/FORMAT.md of the repository.
