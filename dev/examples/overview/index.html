Examples

These pages gather what the package computes for now on the braid groups of reflection groups: the homology with trivial coefficients ℤ and with the sign module ℤε, in which the braid reflections act by −1, and the numbers of cells of the reduced lcm resolutions. They will be completed as the computations progress.

How the values are obtained

For each group, the reduced lcm resolution of a Garside monoid of the braid group, then its certificate and its homology with ℤ and ℤε, the elementary divisors being computed by SparseElementaryDivisors.jl. The monoid is the dual braid monoid B*(W), for the Coxeter element chosen by default, and also the classical braid monoid B⁺(W) for the real groups.

The values are those of the files test/data/expected/*.toml of the repository. The long tests of the package recompute them (environment variable GARSIDE_HOMOLOGY_LONG_TESTS), and the tables of these pages are generated from these files at each build of the site. The groups are written torsion first, as in the tables of Garnier, and ? marks a value not computed yet.

The numbers of cells are those of the reduced lcm resolution of B*(W) in each degree. In all these cases, this resolution is minimal, so that these numbers do not depend on the choices of the construction.

The pages

  • Exceptional braid groups: the 26 well-generated exceptional groups.
  • Spherical Artin groups: the braid groups of the finite Coxeter groups.
  • Imprimitive braid groups: the groups G(m, m, n) and G(m, 1, n).

Not computed yet

With ℤε, the coefficients of the elimination outgrow Int128, the largest integer type used in these computations, for G₃₇ (see Exceptional braid groups) and for:

  • G(m, m, 6) for m = 29
  • G(m, m, 7) for m = 7–28, 30
  • G(m, m, 8) for m = 3–7

Not computed yet either: the eight exceptional groups that are not well-generated, the imprimitive groups beyond the ranges of Imprimitive braid groups, and the homology with coefficients in the Laurent polynomials k[t, t⁻¹], in which the braid reflections act by t.
