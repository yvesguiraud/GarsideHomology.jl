Spherical Artin groups

The braid groups of the finite Coxeter groups, computed from the classical braid monoid B⁺(W) and from the dual braid monoid B*(W) wherever both were computed, with equal results. The types H₃, F₄, H₄, E₆, E₇ and E₈ are also G₂₃, G₂₈, G₃₀, G₃₅, G₃₆ and G₃₇ of the page Exceptional braid groups.
